Tether

Keeps Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Crunchyroll, and Max in sync between two people, anywhere, plus a shared notes pad and chat.

Download Tether

This installs directly in Chrome. It's not on the Chrome Web Store yet, that submission is still in review. Once it's approved, this page will link there instead.

Setup (about 2 minutes)

  1. Click the button above. The file will land in your Downloads folder.
  2. Double-click the downloaded file to unzip it. You'll get a folder named tether-extension.
  3. Open Chrome. Type chrome://extensions into the address bar and press Enter.
  4. Turn on Developer mode (a toggle in the top-right corner).
  5. Click Load unpacked, and select the tether-extension folder from step 2.
  6. Click the Tether icon that appears in your toolbar. If you were sent a room code, paste it in under "Joining someone else's room" and click Join.
  7. Open Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Crunchyroll, or Max and press play, it'll stay in sync from there.
